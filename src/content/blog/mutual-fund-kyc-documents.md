KYC is the one-time identity check you must complete before your first mutual fund investment. Done right, you never think about it again. Done wrong, it shows up years later as "KYC On Hold" right when you want to redeem.

## What you need

- **PAN card.** Mandatory for every investor.
- **Aadhaar.** Used as proof of identity and address. It must be **linked to your PAN**; an unlinked PAN becomes inoperative and will block your KYC.
- **A photo or live selfie**, taken during the online process.
- **Your own mobile number and email**, which will be verified with an OTP.
- **Bank account details** (a cancelled cheque or statement). This is for your folio, so redemptions land in your account.

For a minor, the child's details plus the parent or guardian's KYC are needed. Old-style proofs like electricity bills or bank statements are no longer accepted as KYC address proof.

## How to do it on your phone

1. Start the KYC through your distributor, a fund house website, or a KRA (KYC Registration Agency).
2. Enter your PAN and verify your mobile and email with OTPs.
3. Fetch your Aadhaar details using **Aadhaar OTP or DigiLocker**.
4. Take a live photo, and sign on screen if asked.
5. Submit. You'll usually get a confirmation within one to three working days.

The whole thing takes about ten minutes. At Shaiv Market I do this with investors on a video call, so you don't have to work out the forms alone.

## Check your KYC status (MF KYC status)

Your KYC is held by a **KRA** (KYC Registration Agency). SEBI-registered KRAs include **CAMS KRA, CVL KRA, KFintech KRA, NDML and DotEx (NSE)**. They share records, so any one of them shows your status.

1. Open the KYC inquiry page of any KRA (for example CAMS KRA or CVL KRA).
2. Enter your PAN (and the captcha).
3. Note two things: the **status** and **which KRA** holds your record. You need the second if you have to fix anything.

You'll see one of these:

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Validated** | Your PAN and Aadhaar have been verified with the issuing authority | Invest in any fund house, no need to repeat KYC |
| **Registered** | Your KYC was done with documents that couldn't be verified directly | Existing investments continue; new investments may need you to re-submit KYC with Aadhaar |
| **On Hold** | Something didn't check out: an invalid document, unverified mobile or email, or a PAN problem | Transactions are blocked until you fix it |

## What "KRA validation" means and how to get Validated

Since 2024, KRAs check your PAN, Aadhaar-based address, mobile number and email against official sources. If all of them check out, your status is **Validated**. If your KYC was done years ago with paper documents, it may show **Registered** or **On Hold** until you re-verify.

To move to Validated:

1. Find which KRA holds your record (from the status check above).
2. On that KRA's website (for example the CAMS KRA or NDML client-initiated KYC page), verify your **mobile and email with OTPs**.
3. If asked, **re-do KYC with Aadhaar** (OTP or DigiLocker). This is a KYC modification, not a new KYC.
4. Check again after a few working days.

Your existing investments are not sold or affected while you do this.

## How to do or update KYC online

- **New KYC:** through your distributor, a fund house website or a KRA, using Aadhaar OTP or DigiLocker plus a live photo. This is often called **eKYC** for mutual funds.
- **Update mobile, email or address in your KYC:** submit a KYC modification through your KRA, your fund house or your distributor. Aadhaar-based address updates are the quickest.
- **Update contact details in your folios:** after the KYC is updated, change them across fund houses in one go on **MF Central**.

## KYC documents for an LLP, company or HUF

Non-individual investors complete KYC for the entity and for the people who sign for it. Typically:

- PAN of the entity, and proof of its address
- Incorporation document: LLP agreement and certificate of incorporation, company's certificate and MOA/AOA, or HUF declaration
- List of partners or directors, and a resolution authorising the signatories
- Individual KYC (PAN, Aadhaar, photo) of each authorised signatory, and beneficial-owner details

Exact lists vary a little by fund house, so check before you start.

## How to fix "On Hold"

Most cases come down to one of these:

- **Mobile or email not verified.** Update and verify them through the KRA or your fund house.
- **Old documents.** Redo the KYC using Aadhaar, which usually moves you straight to Validated.
- **PAN-Aadhaar not linked.** Link them on the income tax e-filing portal, wait for it to reflect, then update KYC.
- **Name mismatch.** If the name on your folio doesn't exactly match your PAN (even a missing middle name), correct it, because PAN checks now only return a yes or no on the match.

## Two habits that save trouble later

- **Add a nominee to every folio.** It takes two minutes now and saves your family months of paperwork later.
- **Keep your mobile and email current.** Every transaction confirmation goes there, and it's your first line of defence against fraud.

## Questions people ask

### What documents are required for mutual fund KYC?
PAN, Aadhaar linked to your PAN, a photo or live selfie, and a verified mobile number and email. Bank account details are also needed to open the folio.

### How do I check my mutual fund KYC status?
Visit any KRA's KYC inquiry page, such as CAMS KRA or CVL KRA, and enter your PAN. The status will show as Validated, Registered or On Hold, along with which KRA holds your record.

### What is CAMS KRA KYC validation?
It is the process by which a KRA, here CAMS KRA, verifies your PAN, Aadhaar-based address, mobile and email with official sources. If they match, your status becomes Validated and you can invest with any fund house without repeating KYC.

### How do I get my KYC from Registered to Validated?
Go to the KRA that holds your record, verify your mobile and email with OTPs, and if asked, redo KYC with Aadhaar OTP or DigiLocker. The status usually updates within a few working days.

### How do I update my mutual fund KYC online?
Submit a KYC modification through your KRA, fund house or distributor, using Aadhaar for address changes. Then update mobile and email across your folios on MF Central.

### Is eKYC enough to invest in mutual funds?
Yes, Aadhaar-based online KYC with OTP or DigiLocker and a live photo is accepted. Once your status is Validated, you can invest with any fund house.

### What KYC documents does an LLP need for mutual funds?
The LLP's PAN and address proof, LLP agreement and certificate of incorporation, list of designated partners, a resolution authorising signatories, and individual KYC of each signatory. Check the exact list with the fund house.

### Why is my mutual fund KYC on hold?
Usually because a document could not be verified, your mobile number or email is not verified, your PAN is not linked to Aadhaar, or your folio name doesn't match your PAN. Re-doing KYC with Aadhaar and verifying your contact details fixes most cases.
