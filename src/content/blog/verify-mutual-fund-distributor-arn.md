Anyone can print "mutual fund consultant" on a visiting card. Only someone registered with AMFI can legally distribute mutual funds, and checking takes less time than making tea.

## ARN meaning and full form

**ARN** stands for **AMFI Registration Number**. AMFI is the Association of Mutual Funds in India. A person or firm gets an ARN only after passing the NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors) certification and registering with AMFI. It must be renewed every three years. It looks like **ARN-337289** (that one's mine).

Without a valid ARN, nobody can sell mutual funds to you or earn commission from a fund house.

## ARN vs EUIN

| | ARN | EUIN |
|---|---|---|
| Full form | AMFI Registration Number | Employee Unique Identification Number |
| Who has it | The distributor: an individual or a firm | The individual person who deals with you (employee, relationship manager, or the individual distributor) |
| Why it exists | Licence to distribute mutual funds | Traces each sale to the person who made it |
| Example | ARN-337289 | E641362 |

When you invest through a distributor, the application form carries both: the ARN, and the EUIN of the person who handled the sale.

## How to use the AMFI ARN locator

1. Go to **amfiindia.com** and open **Locate Distributor**. This is what people call the "ARN locator" or "ARN search".
2. Search by **ARN**. You can also search by the holder's name, city, PIN code or local area to find distributors near you.
3. Check three things:
   - **Name** matches the person or firm you are dealing with.
   - **Validity date** is in the future. An expired ARN cannot earn commission on new business.
   - **City and address** make sense for where they say they work.

If the ARN doesn't show up, or the name is different, stop there. AMFI also publishes a list of **suspended or terminated ARNs** on the same Locate Distributor section; it is worth a look if anything feels off.

## How to find your distributor's ARN and EUIN

Already invested and not sure who your distributor is?

- **Consolidated Account Statement (CAS):** download it from CAMS, KFintech or MF Central with your PAN and email. Each folio or transaction shows the distributor's ARN (often under a "Broker" or "Advisor" column). "Direct" means no distributor is linked.
- **Application forms and transaction slips:** the ARN and EUIN are written in the distributor boxes at the top of the form.
- **Ask your distributor:** a genuine one will share both numbers immediately. Then check the ARN on the AMFI locator.

To change who services your existing investments, you can file a change of broker request; your units stay in your name and nothing is sold.

## Red flags

- **"Guaranteed" returns on equity funds.** Nobody can guarantee market returns.
- **Payments to a personal or company account.** Your money should only go to the mutual fund's own account, through BSE StAR MF, NSE, MF Central or the fund house's website.
- **Requests for OTPs, net banking passwords or signed blank forms.**
- **Calling themselves an "adviser", "IFA" or "wealth manager"** without a SEBI investment adviser registration. Distributors aren't allowed to.
- **Constant switching** between funds or NFO pushing without a clear reason tied to your goals.

## Confirm your money actually arrived

Even with a genuine distributor, verify every transaction yourself:

- After each purchase you should get an **SMS and email from the fund house or its registrar** (CAMS or KFintech), not just a WhatsApp from the distributor.
- Download your **Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)** for free from CAMS, KFintech or **MF Central**. It lists every folio and unit in your PAN across all fund houses.
- Your distributor's ARN appears against each investment in the statement. That's how you know who is linked to your account.

## How a distributor gets an ARN

For anyone who wants to become a mutual fund distributor:

1. Pass the **NISM Series V-A** Mutual Fund Distributors certification exam.
2. Complete **KYD** (Know Your Distributor) verification.
3. Apply to AMFI for registration; the ARN is issued after checks.
4. Renew every three years, keeping the NISM certification current.

Individuals and firms (partnerships, companies) can both hold an ARN. A firm's employees who deal with investors get their own EUIN.

## If something goes wrong

Raise it first with the fund house. If it isn't resolved, you can complain to SEBI through its online grievance system, **SCORES**, and to AMFI for issues involving a distributor.

## Questions people ask

### What is ARN in mutual funds?
ARN is the AMFI Registration Number, the registration every mutual fund distributor in India must hold. It is issued by AMFI after the person passes the NISM Series V-A exam, and it must be renewed every three years.

### What is the full form of ARN?
AMFI Registration Number. AMFI stands for the Association of Mutual Funds in India.

### What is the ARN locator?
It is the "Locate Distributor" page on amfiindia.com. You can search by ARN, name, city or PIN code to check a distributor's registration and validity date, or to find registered distributors near you.

### How do I find my EUIN number?
The EUIN of the person who handled your investment is written next to the distributor's ARN on the application form or transaction slip. If you can't find it, ask your distributor; they must share it.

### How do I get an ARN number?
Pass the NISM Series V-A exam, complete Know Your Distributor (KYD) verification, and apply to AMFI for registration. The ARN must be renewed every three years.

### How can I check if my mutual fund agent is genuine?
Search their ARN on the Locate Distributor page of amfiindia.com and confirm the name and validity date. Also make sure all payments go to the mutual fund's account and that you receive confirmations directly from the fund house or registrar.

### What is the difference between ARN and EUIN?
ARN is the AMFI Registration Number of the distributor (a person or firm). EUIN is a unique number for an individual employee or relationship manager working under a distributor firm, so each transaction can be traced to the person who handled it.

### Where can I see all my mutual fund investments in one place?
Download a Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS, KFintech or MF Central using your PAN and email. It shows all your folios across fund houses.
