## How the numbers are worked out

Every calculator here uses the same conventions, so you can move between them without the answers drifting apart:

- **SIPs are invested at the start of each month** and compound monthly, at one-twelfth of the yearly return you choose. Most fund house calculators work the same way.
- **Lumpsums and PPF compound once a year.**
- **Returns are constant.** Real markets never deliver a smooth 12% every year. Treat each result as a reasonable estimate, then try a lower return to see how sensitive the plan is.
- **Tax is not deducted** unless the calculator says so. The mutual fund tax calculator does the tax part on its own.

If another website gives a slightly different figure for the same inputs, it is almost always because it assumes end-of-month instalments or yearly compounding. The difference is small and does not change any decision.

## Questions people ask

### Are these calculators free to use?
Yes. There is no sign-up, no payment and no limit on how many times you use them.

### Do you save the numbers I enter?
No. The calculations run in your own browser. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to us unless you choose to fill a contact or booking form.

### Why do different SIP calculators give slightly different answers?
Because of small assumptions: whether each instalment is invested at the start or end of the month, and whether returns compound monthly or yearly. These calculators assume start-of-month instalments with monthly compounding.

### What return should I assume for equity mutual funds?
No one can know future returns. Many planners use 10 to 12% a year for diversified equity funds over long periods and 6 to 7% for debt funds, and then check the plan at a lower number too. Past returns do not guarantee future ones.

### Can I use these calculators on my phone?
Yes. Every calculator works on a phone. You can type amounts like "5 lakh" or "1.5 crore" straight into the boxes.
